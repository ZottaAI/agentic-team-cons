# Standards pointers (you supply the content)

This template ships the *shape* of a Risk Advisory engagement, not any firm's proprietary
methodology. Point the agents and skills at your own authoritative sources. Put your firm's
methodology references, internal manuals, and current regulatory extracts in `.claude/memory/`
and reference them here.

Common anchors by service line (verify currency against the primary source before relying on them):

| Service line | Anchors |
|---|---|
| Internal Audit | IIA Global Internal Audit Standards (2024, effective 2025); Three Lines Model |
| Controls / SOX / ICFR | COSO 2013 (5 components, 17 principles); PCAOB AS 2201 concepts; ITGC frameworks |
| Regulatory & Compliance | FCA Handbook (PRIN, SYSC, COBS, Consumer Duty); PRA Rulebook; SMCR |
| Cyber & Technology Risk | NIST CSF 2.0; ISO/IEC 27001:2022; CIS Controls v8 |
| Financial Crime / AML | MLR 2017; JMLSG Guidance; FCA Financial Crime Guide; POCA; sanctions regimes |
| Operational Resilience | PRA SS1/21; FCA PS21/3 |
| Data & Privacy | UK GDPR; Data Protection Act 2018; ICO guidance |
| Third-Party / EERM | PRA SS2/21; EBA outsourcing guidelines |

Firm-wide quality and ethics:
- ISQM 1 (firm system of quality management), ISQM 2 (engagement quality reviews), ISA 220 (revised).
- FRC Ethical Standard.

> These are pointers, not the methodology. Do not embed proprietary content in this template.
> Always confirm the current version of any standard or rule against the issuing body.
