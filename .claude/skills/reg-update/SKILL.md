# /reg-update — current regulatory position

Dispatch the **knowledge** agent. For the engagement's scope and service line, surface the current
position and any recent change.

## Look for
- Policy statements, consultation outcomes, and rule changes from the relevant regulator.
- Dear CEO / portfolio letters and supervisory priorities that signal where the regulator is looking.
- Enforcement themes and decision notices relevant to the scope.
- For data work, ICO guidance and enforcement; for resilience, PRA/FCA resilience publications; etc.

## Output
A short, sourced brief of the current position and anything that changed recently and is relevant.
Cite primary sources.

## Hard rules
- Cite sources. Do not assert a regulatory position without a reference.
- If your knowledge may be out of date, say so and recommend verifying against the primary source.
  Regulatory currency is a real risk; do not over-claim it.
- This informs the work; the technical-reviewer agent owns the technical conclusion.
