# /escalate — mandatory consultation

Use when a matter is contentious, novel, high-risk, or outside standard methodology, or when
independence is in question. Do not let an agent quietly resolve a hard call in the file.

## Steps
1. State the matter crisply: the question, why it is hard, the options, and the team's provisional view.
2. Route to the right consultee: technical-reviewer / national office for technical matters,
   qrm-independence for independence and ethics, partner for commercial-risk calls.
3. Record the consultation and the conclusion in `engagements/{ref}/consultations.md`, including who
   was consulted and the basis for the resolution.
4. Reflect the resolution in the workpapers and, if relevant, the gate-log.

## Output
A documented consultation with a clear resolution that the file now follows.

## Hard rules
- Uncertainty is a reason to consult, not a reason to wave a matter through.
- The consultation record is part of the defensible file. "We discussed it" is not enough; record the
  question, who was consulted, and why the conclusion holds.
