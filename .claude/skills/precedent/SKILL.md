# /precedent — learn from what the firm already knows

Dispatch the **knowledge** agent. Search `.claude/memory/` and `templates/` for prior engagements of
the same type or sector.

## Retrieve and summarise
- The approach and programme used last time.
- The findings patterns that recurred (so the team looks in the right places).
- What was contentious or got challenged at EQCR (so it is not relearned the hard way).
- The right templates and standards pointers for this engagement type.

## Output
A short, sourced brief: what was found, where it came from, and why it matters here. Cite every source.
Distinguish internal firm precedent from external material.

## Hard rules
- Read-only. Inform the team; do not make the judgement calls.
- Share other-client precedent as anonymised approach and pattern only, never as another client's data.
  Respect information barriers.
