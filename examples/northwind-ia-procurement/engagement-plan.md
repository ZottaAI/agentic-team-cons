- **Manager:** S. Okafor     **Date:** 2026-05-14
- **Service-line tag(s):** internal-audit

## Objectives
Provide the Board Audit Committee with assurance over the design and operating effectiveness of
controls in the procurement-to-pay process, with specific focus on whether unauthorised or fraudulent
supplier payments can be made, given the Q1 2026 near-miss.

## Scope
- **In scope:** supplier onboarding and master-data changes; purchase-order and three-way match;
  payment authorisation including the **urgent / manual payment path**; payment release.
- **Out of scope:** payroll; treasury / wholesale payments; vendor commercial terms; the GL close.
- **Scope limitations:** testing covers the period 2025-10-01 to 2026-04-30; one decommissioned
  legacy payment queue (retired Feb 2026) is examined for design only.

## Risk universe
R1 unauthorised supplier payment; R2 duplicate / inaccurate payment; R3 fraudulent change of supplier
bank details; R4 override of preventive controls without justification.

## Approach
Walkthroughs to confirm design, then tests of operating effectiveness over the period. Data analytics
on the full payment population to target samples on the urgent path and on control overrides. IA pack
applies IIA Global Internal Audit Standards 2024.
