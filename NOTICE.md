# Notice & disclaimer

**What this is.** A generic, firm-agnostic template for running a Risk Advisory engagement with an
AI team and human review gates. It encodes the *shape* of a consulting engagement (roles, stages,
quality gates, artefacts), not any specific firm's methodology.

**Not affiliated.** This template is not produced by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Deloitte or any
other firm. References to standards (IIA, COSO, ISQM, FRC, FCA, PRA, ICO, NIST, ISO) are pointers to
publicly identifiable frameworks for orientation only. It contains no proprietary methodology.

**Not professional advice.** Nothing here is legal, audit, accounting, regulatory, or other
professional advice. The outputs of these agents are drafts and aids, not conclusions. A qualified
human remains responsible for every judgement and every sign-off.

**Human accountability is built in by design.** Gates G3 (quality) and G4 (sign-off) require named
human reviewers. The AI never signs off. Adapters must preserve this. Do not configure any agent to
approve, opine, or perform a management function on behalf of a human, especially for an audit client.

**Confidentiality.** Do not route client-confidential data, PII, or material non-public information to
any model or tool that is not approved for that data classification. The `/confidentiality-check`
skill exists to enforce this. Per-engagement working files are gitignored for this reason.

**Currency.** Standards and regulations change. Always verify the current version against the issuing
body before relying on any reference in this template.

Use at your own risk. Adapt to your firm's actual methodology, quality system, and regulatory
obligations before any real engagement.
